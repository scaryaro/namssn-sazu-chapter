About NAMSSN SAZU

The National Association of Mathematical Sciences Students of Nigeria — SAZU Chapter

OUR STORY

Who We Are

NAMSSN (National Association of Mathematical Sciences Students of Nigeria) SAZU Chapter is the student body representing all mathematical sciences students at SAZU. We are affiliated with the national body of NAMSSN, which spans universities across Nigeria.

Our chapter brings together students from the Department of Mathematics to foster academic growth, professional networking, and a sense of community. Through our events, competitions, and welfare initiatives, we strive to create an environment where every student can thrive.

What Drives Us

Our mission, vision, and core values

Mission

To promote academic excellence, professional development, and unity among mathematical sciences students at SAZU.

Vision

To be the leading student association shaping the future of mathematics and its applications in Nigeria and beyond.

Values

Excellence, integrity, collaboration, innovation, and service to the mathematical sciences community.

Impact

Organising competitions, workshops, and social events that prepare our members for successful careers in STEM fields.

The Mathematics Department

The Department of Mathematics at SAZU is one of the oldest and most respected mathematics departments. It offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in Mathematics, Statistics, and related fields, producing graduates who excel in academia, industry, and public service.
